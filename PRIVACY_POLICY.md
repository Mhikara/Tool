# Privacy Policy for Super Tools

**Last Updated:** [Date]

Super Tools ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We adhere strictly to Google Play's Data Safety requirements.

## 1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

Super Tools is designed to function primarily locally on your device. We prioritize your privacy and limit data collection. The App requests the following permissions for specific features:

### 1.1 Device and Installed App Information
To provide features like the App Scanner, Permission Manager, and Device Diagnostics, the App needs to query information about the applications installed on your device (`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`).
- **How it is used:** This data is used *strictly locally* on your device to display app details, analyze permissions, and identify potential security risks.
- **Data Sharing:** This information is **never** transmitted to our servers or shared with any third parties.

### 1.2 Storage Access
Features like the File Manager require access to your device's storage (`MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` and Media permissions).
- **How it is used:** Used solely to allow you to browse, manage, and organize files on your device.
- **Data Sharing:** We do not collect, read, or upload your files to our servers. All file management operations occur locally.

### 1.3 Camera and Microphone
The App may request access to your camera and microphone (`CAMERA`, `RECORD_AUDIO`).
- **How it is used:** Used for features such as scanning QR codes, taking photos, or recording audio/video directly within the tools provided.
- **Data Sharing:** Media captured is processed locally and saved to your device. It is not collected by us.

### 1.4 Network and Internet Access
The App requires internet access and network state information (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`).
- **How it is used:** Used to verify internet connectivity, interact with official third-party APIs (such as Gemini AI for Support AI), and upload your content to linked accounts.
- **Data Security:** All network communications with external services are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

### 1.5 Background Processing (WorkManager)
The App utilizes Android's WorkManager for reliable background execution.
- **How it is used:** Used to perform long-running tasks you initiate, such as the Super Auto Upload feature, even if you close the App. This ensures your tasks complete successfully without requiring the App to stay open.

### 1.6 Media Uploads (Super Auto Upload)
When you use the Super Auto Upload feature to upload content to YouTube or TikTok, the App uses official OAuth authentication.
- **How it is used:** We request only the permissions necessary to upload the specific files you choose. Authentication tokens are stored securely and locally.
- **Data Sharing:** The content you upload is sent directly to YouTube or TikTok through their official APIs. We do not intercept or store this content.

We implement robust security measures:
- **Local Processing:** Most sensitive data is processed locally on your device.
- **Encryption in Transit:** Any data transmitted to authorized third-party APIs is encrypted.
- **No Cloud Storage:** We do not maintain servers to store your personal data, files, or app usage history.
